## Your Music. Your Metadata.

Take control of your library. Edit the information stored inside your files without leaving Playnck.

<img src="docs/screenshots/Edit.png" alt="Edit Track dialog with cover artwork, title, artist, album, genre, and release year" width="100%">

- Full metadata editing, written into the real file
- Cover artwork
- Genre and release information
- Track and disc numbers
- Advanced fields: composer, lyricist, conductor, label, ISRC, BPM and more

<br>

## Let Playnck Identify It

Don't know what's in that file? Hit **Identify from audio** in the editor above and let Playnck figure it out.

- Audio fingerprinting
- Search by title and artist
- Compare matches and pick the right one
- Cover artwork included
- Review before you save

<br>

## Sing Along

Time-synced lyrics that follow the song. Running early or late? Nudge the timing until it's right.
